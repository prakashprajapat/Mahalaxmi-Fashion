Automatic SMS to the customer about their own order

Seven DLT templates, each on its own trigger, so a customer hears what is
happening to their parcel without anybody typing anything.

Five are sent where the thing happens:

  OrderConfirm    PlaceOrder, once the order row exists
  Tracking        the moment a waybill is generated, manual or automatic
  OutForDelivery  the tracking sweep, when the courier says it is on a van
  Delivered       the tracking sweep, when the courier says it arrived
  Refund          an RTO that comes home owing money, and only when owed > 0

Two are sent by a clock, in the new CustomerMessageService, hourly:

  Review          an hour after delivery, then twice more 48 hours apart
  UserDropped     an hour after somebody reached the payment page and did not pay

message_sends, and why it is the whole feature
----------------------------------------------
One row per (order, template, attempt), with a unique index, written BEFORE
the send rather than after. The tracking sweep runs every three hours and
re-reads the same courier status each time: without the row, a parcel out for
delivery all day tells the customer so eight times, and a delivered order asks
for a review every three hours until somebody notices. The index, not a
read-then-write, is what enforces "once" - two sweeps racing each other both
see no row and both decide to send; only one gets to write.

A failed send is recorded as failed, not deleted. A number MSG91 refuses is
refused every time, and retrying for ever is how one bad number becomes a
daily error. An order with no phone is recorded too, for the same reason.

What the sweep refuses to send
------------------------------
  - a review ask, once a review exists for that order, whatever the count says
  - a review ask after three, ever
  - "your payment did not go through" to a Cashfree PENDING row. The bank is
    still deciding; that message, sent while it succeeds, loses a sale that was
    already won
  - anything to a dropped checkout that later became an order - paid on a
    second attempt, written by the webhook, or switched to Cash on Delivery
  - more than one UserDropped per phone per day. Three attempts at the same
    payment is one person having trouble, not three sales to chase, and the
    unique index cannot catch it: three rows, three order ids, one person
  - Tracking on a reverse-pickup waybill. That AWB is the shop's parcel coming
    home, not the customer's parcel going out

Both sweeps put every condition inside the query rather than filtering after
the 25-row batch. The replay caught why: oldest-first, on a day with sixty
deliveries the first 25 are asked and then fill the batch on every sweep for
the next five days - skipped each time, but occupying the room. Customers 26
to 60 would never be asked at all, and their window would close while the
sweep reported success.

Settings
--------
A new section holds the seven MSG91 flow ids and a master switch. A blank box
means that one message is not sent and nothing else changes. Turning the
review emails off turns the review texts off with them - a switch that half
works is worse than none.

The order and payment emails to a second address need no code: AdminNotifier
already splits admin_email on commas.
